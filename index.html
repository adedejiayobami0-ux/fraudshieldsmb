FAQ

A different kind of FraudShield.

Is FraudShield replacing Stripe Radar?

No. FraudShield is being designed as a broader business risk and operations layer. Payment-processor signals can eventually become one input among many.

Does FraudShield do my accounting?

FraudShield can assist with understanding, categorization, summaries, and questions. It should not pretend to replace professional accounting or tax advice.

What can I use today?

The current beta includes the existing FraudShield web experience. The new @fraud and @accounting workflows are the next product layer being built.

Why a browser extension?

Because business risk appears inside email, accounting tools, commerce platforms, and payment dashboards. The extension can reduce context switching.
