Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 30, 2026

1. Overview

FraudShield SMB (“FraudShield,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides an AI business protection and operations workspace. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use FraudShield, including when you connect third-party services such as Google Gmail.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, profile information, business name, and authentication details.
  • Usage information: conversations, selected agents, uploaded files, feature usage, credit activity, and technical logs needed to operate and secure the service.
  • Billing information: subscription status, Stripe customer/subscription identifiers, purchase history, and credit transactions. Payment card details are processed by Stripe and are not stored by FraudShield.
  • Connected-service data: if you choose to connect Gmail or another integration, FraudShield may access only the data permitted by the scopes you authorize.

3. Google user data and Gmail

If you connect your Google account, FraudShield requests read-only Gmail access so you can ask FraudShield to inspect emails for potential fraud, suspicious requests, vendor risk, phishing indicators, and related business-protection use cases.

FraudShield does not use Gmail access to send, delete, modify, or permanently archive your emails. You control whether your Google account is connected, and you may revoke access at any time through your Google Account permissions.

FraudShield's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. How we use information

  • Provide and improve FraudShield's AI analysis and business workflows.
  • Process requests you initiate, including fraud checks and document/email analysis.
  • Maintain accounts, subscriptions, credits, and customer support.
  • Protect the service against abuse, fraud, unauthorized access, and security threats.
  • Understand product performance and troubleshoot errors.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

5. AI processing

Content you submit may be transmitted to AI model providers or infrastructure providers as needed to generate a response. We use service providers only to operate FraudShield and do not sell your conversations, uploaded documents, or connected Gmail content to advertisers.

6. How we share information

We may share information with service providers that help us operate FraudShield, such as cloud hosting, authentication, AI processing, analytics, and payment processing providers. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect users or the service.

We do not sell personal information or Google user data.

7. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide FraudShield, maintain security and billing records, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes. You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information, subject to records we are legally required to retain.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including authentication, access controls, private storage, database row-level security, rate limits, and server-side handling of sensitive service credentials. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices

  • You may disconnect Google access at any time from your Google Account's third-party access settings.
  • You may cancel a paid FraudShield subscription through the billing portal.
  • You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information where applicable.

10. Children's privacy

FraudShield is intended for business users and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as FraudShield evolves. We will post the revised version on this page and update the effective date when changes are material.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, account deletion requests, or questions about connected Google data, contact FraudShield through the support channel available on our website.
