Clear boundaries

FraudShield helps you review. It does not make the final decision for you.

AI-generated insights are informational. Important financial, security, accounting, legal, or tax decisions should be verified using the original records and, where appropriate, qualified professionals.

Not proof of fraud

A risk flag or unusual pattern is a review signal, not a definitive finding that fraud occurred.

Not an accountant

FraudShield can explain and summarize accounting information, but it does not replace professional accounting or tax advice.

Not legal advice

Document and compliance-related assistance is informational and does not determine legal sufficiency or provide legal advice.

Does not move money

FraudShield analyzes available payment and financial information. It is not a bank, lender, payment processor, or money-transfer service.

Gmail stays read-only

FraudShield can read authorized Gmail data for analysis but does not send, delete, move, label, or otherwise modify email messages.

User verification still matters

Before paying an invoice, changing banking information, or taking another consequential action, verify the underlying information independently.

Privacy & security

Built around user-authorized access.

FraudShield is designed so connected business information is associated with the authenticated user who authorized access. Users can disconnect supported integrations when they no longer want FraudShield to access them.

Connection controls

Connected services use OAuth authorization. Credentials and server-side integration secrets are kept out of browser-facing application code.

Optional MFA

Users can enable TOTP multi-factor authentication from account security settings for additional account protection.

QuickBooks token protection

QuickBooks OAuth tokens are stored encrypted server-side and are refreshed through controlled backend flows when necessary.

Your business context

Connected data is used to provide FraudShield features to the authenticated customer and is not presented as another customer's business data.
